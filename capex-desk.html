Why boards call us

Volunteer boards. Million-dollar decisions.

Milestone inspections and structural integrity reserve studies are pushing Florida associations into large repair projects. Owners can't vote to waive the work, and the bill often arrives as a special assessment. Most board members have never leveled a bid or reviewed a pay application — and they're the ones who sign.

01

The low bid isn't low

Bids that exclude concrete quantities, permits, or general conditions look cheaper on paper — then come back as change orders once the contract is signed.

02

The assessment is a guess

Boards are asked to vote on a seven-figure assessment built from an engineer's estimate, with no contingency logic and no market check on the numbers.

03

Pay requests run ahead of the work

Without checking billed percentages against actual progress, associations pay for work that isn't done — and lose their leverage before closeout.

    Questions

    Before you send.

    Who actually does the review?

    Every review is done by Hugo Delgado, a PMP certified owner's representative and Florida-licensed community association manager with over a decade managing capital projects for South Florida associations. Nothing is outsourced.

    Do you need to visit the property?

    No. CapEx Desk reviews are document-based: bids, specifications, estimates, contracts, pay applications, and photos. If the project needs someone on site, DRXL MGMT's full owner's representation covers that in Miami-Dade and Broward.

    Is this an engineering opinion?

    No. We review cost, scope, contract terms, and progress. Structural adequacy, milestone inspections, and repair design are the work of a Florida-licensed engineer or architect. If a finding needs engineering input, the report says so.

    Can our management company order it?

    Yes. Managers often use a review to give the board an independent read before a vote. The report is addressed to the association either way.

    Do you work with contractors?

    We have no financial relationships with contractors, vendors, or suppliers — no referral fees and no preferred lists. That's what keeps a review independent.

    Are the reports available in Spanish?

    Yes. Ask for English, Spanish, or both — at no extra cost.

    How do we pay?

    Once you approve the confirmed scope, we email an invoice payable by card or bank transfer. Work starts when documents are complete.
