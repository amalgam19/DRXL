Biography

A PMP certified construction project manager and South Florida native with over a decade of boots-on-the-ground experience managing capital projects across Miami-Dade and Broward County. Ground-up builds, commercial renovations, envelope restorations, drainage systems, reroofing — the full spectrum of construction project delivery.

DRXL was founded on a simple observation: most owners — whether a condo board, a developer, or a private client — lack the construction management expertise to hold their contractors, engineers, and consultants truly accountable. Budgets drift. Schedules slip. Change orders pile up. DRXL exists to close that gap with structured, experienced, independent oversight.

What makes the DRXL approach different is the combination of certified construction PM discipline and deep community association knowledge. The PMP credential brings rigorous project controls. The LCAM license brings governance fluency. Together, they cover every dimension of a South Florida capital project — from preconstruction through compliance.

When you work with DRXL, you're not hiring a generalist. You're hiring a certified construction manager who has been in the field, knows this market, and is solely committed to protecting your interests from day one.

Florida Compliance

Navigating Florida's
regulatory landscape.

01

40 & 50-Year Recertification

Florida law requires structural and electrical recertifications for buildings 40 years and older. We manage the entire process — engineer coordination, inspection scheduling, repair scoping, contractor procurement, and permit closeout.

  • Engineer selection & coordination
  • Inspection & report management
  • Repair scope & bid procurement
  • Permit & closeout management

02

Reserve Studies & SIRS

Florida's Structural Integrity Reserve Study (SIRS) legislation requires condominium associations to fund reserves for critical building components. We help boards understand their obligations and translate findings into actionable capital plans.

  • Reserve specialist coordination
  • SIRS compliance review
  • Capital plan development
  • Board presentation support

03

Post-Surfside Compliance

Florida's SB 4-D requires milestone inspections, structural integrity reserve studies, and mandatory reserve funding. We guide boards through every compliance requirement — clearly, efficiently, and without the legal jargon.

  • Milestone inspection management
  • SB 4-D compliance guidance
  • Structural repair oversight
  • Documentation & reporting
